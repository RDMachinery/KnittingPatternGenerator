Fair Isle Diamonds

Large outlined diamonds with a solid centre, separated by small diamonds where the repeats meet. Knitted in the round in two colours.

Cast on a multiple of 12 stitches and join to work in the round. The pattern repeats every 12 rounds. Read the chart from the bottom up; every round is read from right to left.

Instructions

Round 1*(k2 B, k3 A) twice, k2 B; repeat from * to end.
Round 2*k2 B, (k2 A, k1 B) twice, k2 A, k2 B; repeat from * to end.
Round 3*k1 B, k2 A, k1 B, k4 A, k1 B, k2 A, k1 B; repeat from * to end.
Round 4*k2 A, k1 B, k2 A, k2 B, k2 A, k1 B, k2 A; repeat from * to end.
Round 5*k1 A, k1 B, k2 A, k4 B, k2 A, k1 B, k1 A; repeat from * to end.
Round 6*k1 B, k3 A, k4 B, k3 A, k1 B; repeat from * to end.
Round 7As round 6.
Round 8As round 5.
Round 9As round 4.
Round 10As round 3.
Round 11As round 2.
Round 12As round 1.

Repeat rounds 1–12 for the length you need.

Notes

Every round is knitted, so every round is read from right to left.

Hold both yarns and strand the one you are not using loosely across the back. The longest float in this motif is 4 stitches, so you don't need to catch any floats.

Stranded knitting is firmer than plain knitting: knit a gauge swatch in the round, and go up a needle size if your fabric puckers.
