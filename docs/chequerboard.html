Chequerboard

Bold two-colour squares, like a chessboard. Simple to knit and very striking on hats, cushions and bags.

Cast on a multiple of 8 stitches and join to work in the round. The pattern repeats every 8 rounds. Read the chart from the bottom up; every round is read from right to left.

Yarns

 Yarn A: Oatmeal
 Yarn B: Forest green

Instructions

Round 1*k4 B, k4 A; repeat from * to end.
Round 2As round 1.
Round 3As round 1.
Round 4As round 1.
Round 5*k4 A, k4 B; repeat from * to end.
Round 6As round 5.
Round 7As round 5.
Round 8As round 5.

Repeat rounds 1–8 for the length you need.

Notes

Knitted in the round: every round is read from right to left.

Each colour is carried across 4 stitches at most, so the floats stay short.

Swap the colours every 4 rounds, with the blocks moving over by 4 stitches.
