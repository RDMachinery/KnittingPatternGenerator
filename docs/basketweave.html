Basketweave

Alternating blocks of knit and purl that look like a woven basket. It lies flat, looks good on both sides, and is very satisfying to knit. Great for dishcloths, blankets and bags.

Cast on a multiple of 8 stitches. The pattern repeats every 8 rows. Read the chart from the bottom up. Right-side (odd) rows are read from right to left, wrong-side (even) rows from left to right.

Key

Knit on right-side rows; purl on wrong-side rows.
Purl on right-side rows; knit on wrong-side rows.

Instructions

Row 1 (RS)*k4, p4; repeat from * to end.
Row 2 (WS)*k4, p4; repeat from * to end.
Row 3 (RS)As row 1.
Row 4 (WS)As row 2.
Row 5 (RS)*p4, k4; repeat from * to end.
Row 6 (WS)*p4, k4; repeat from * to end.
Row 7 (RS)As row 5.
Row 8 (WS)As row 6.

Repeat rows 1–8 for the length you need.

Notes

Knit the knits and purl the purls as they face you. After four rows, swap the blocks over.

For a scarf with matching edges, cast on a multiple of 8 plus 4, and finish each row by working the first 4 stitches of the chart again.
