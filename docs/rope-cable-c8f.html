Rope Cable (C8F)

A bold rope cable: 8 knit stitches crossed every 8 rows, framed by 2 purl stitches each side. Perfect for jumpers and cushions.

This is a panel 12 stitches wide: work it between your other stitches, with a marker each side. It repeats every 8 rows. Read the chart from the bottom up. Right-side (odd) rows are read from right to left, wrong-side (even) rows from left to right.

Key

Knit on right-side rows; purl on wrong-side rows.
Purl on right-side rows; knit on wrong-side rows.
C8F: slip 4 stitches to a cable needle and hold at the front of the work, knit 4, then knit 4 from the cable needle.

Instructions

Row 1 (RS)p2, k8, p2.
Row 2 (WS)k2, p8, k2.
Row 3 (RS)As row 1.
Row 4 (WS)As row 2.
Row 5 (RS)p2, C8F, p2.
Row 6 (WS)As row 2.
Row 7 (RS)As row 1.
Row 8 (WS)As row 2.

Repeat rows 1–8 for the length you need.

Notes

This is a panel: work its 12 stitches wherever you want the cable, with a stitch marker each side.

Crossing every 8 rows gives an open, rounded rope. Cross every 6 rows for a tighter twist.

Cables pull the fabric in, so a cabled piece needs more stitches than a plain one of the same width.
