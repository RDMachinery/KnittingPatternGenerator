Bobble Diamond

Two strands of knit stitches travel apart and back again over a purl background, making a chain of diamonds with a bobble in the middle of each. A classic aran panel.

This is a panel 13 stitches wide: work it between your other stitches, with a marker each side. It repeats every 8 rows. Read the chart from the bottom up. Right-side (odd) rows are read from right to left, wrong-side (even) rows from left to right.

Key

Knit on right-side rows; purl on wrong-side rows.
Purl on right-side rows; knit on wrong-side rows.
Make bobble: (k1, p1, k1, p1, k1) all into the next stitch to make 5 stitches; turn and p5; turn and k5; then pass the 2nd, 3rd, 4th and 5th stitches over the first. 1 stitch becomes a round knob.
T4F (twist 4 front): slip 2 stitches to a cable needle and hold at the front of the work, purl 2, then knit 2 from the cable needle. The knit stitches travel to the left over the purl background.
T4B (twist 4 back): slip 2 stitches to a cable needle and hold at the back of the work, knit 2, then purl 2 from the cable needle. The knit stitches travel to the right over the purl background.

Instructions

Row 1 (RS)p2, T4B, p1, T4F, p2.
Row 2 (WS)k2, p2, k5, p2, k2.
Row 3 (RS)T4B, p2, MB, p2, T4F.
Row 4 (WS)p2, k9, p2.
Row 5 (RS)T4F, p5, T4B.
Row 6 (WS)As row 2.
Row 7 (RS)p2, T4F, p1, T4B, p2.
Row 8 (WS)k4, p2, k1, p2, k4.

Repeat rows 1–8 for the length you need.

Notes

This is a panel: work its 13 stitches wherever you want it, with a stitch marker each side.

On wrong-side rows, knit the knits and purl the purls as they face you.

T4F moves the knit stitches to the left and T4B moves them to the right. Work bobbles firmly so they pop out on the right side.
