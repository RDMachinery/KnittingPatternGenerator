Seed (Moss) Stitch

A bumpy, reversible texture that lies flat and doesn't curl. Called moss stitch in the UK and seed stitch in the US.

Cast on a multiple of 2 stitches. The pattern repeats every 2 rows. Read the chart from the bottom up. Right-side (odd) rows are read from right to left, wrong-side (even) rows from left to right.

Key

Knit on right-side rows; purl on wrong-side rows.
Purl on right-side rows; knit on wrong-side rows.

Instructions

Row 1 (RS)*k1, p1; repeat from * to end.
Row 2 (WS)*p1, k1; repeat from * to end.

Repeat rows 1–2 for the length you need.

Notes

Knit the purls and purl the knits as they face you, so the bumps sit in a checkerboard.

Great for borders on stocking-stitch pieces because it doesn't roll.
