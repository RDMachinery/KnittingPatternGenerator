2×2 Rib

Classic, stretchy ribbing. Ideal for cuffs, collars and hat brims.

Cast on a multiple of 4 stitches. The pattern repeats every 2 rows. Read the chart from the bottom up. Right-side (odd) rows are read from right to left, wrong-side (even) rows from left to right.

Key

Knit on right-side rows; purl on wrong-side rows.
Purl on right-side rows; knit on wrong-side rows.

Instructions

Row 1 (RS)*k2, p2; repeat from * to end.
Row 2 (WS)*k2, p2; repeat from * to end.

Repeat rows 1–2 for the length you need.

Notes

Knit the knits and purl the purls as they face you.

Use a needle about half a size to one size smaller than for the main fabric.

For a scarf with matching edges, cast on a multiple of 4 plus 2 and finish every row with k2.
