Fishtail Lace

Little V shapes like the tails of fish, stacked in columns. A gentle first lace pattern, with every wrong-side row purled.

Cast on a multiple of 8 stitches. The pattern repeats every 6 rows. Read the chart from the bottom up. Right-side (odd) rows are read from right to left, wrong-side (even) rows from left to right.

Key

Knit on right-side rows; purl on wrong-side rows.
Yarn over: take the yarn over the needle to make a new stitch and a small decorative hole.
SK2P: slip 1 knitwise, k2tog, then pass the slipped stitch over. A double decrease: 3 stitches become 1.

Instructions

Row 1 (RS)*yo, k2, sk2p, k2, yo, k1; repeat from * to end.
Row 2 (WS)Purl.
Row 3 (RS)*k1, yo, k1, sk2p, k1, yo, k2; repeat from * to end.
Row 4 (WS)As row 2.
Row 5 (RS)*k2, yo, sk2p, yo, k3; repeat from * to end.
Row 6 (WS)As row 2.

Repeat rows 1–6 for the length you need.

Notes

Each right-side row is balanced: two yarn overs make up for the two stitches lost in the SK2P.

For a symmetrical edge, cast on a multiple of 8 plus 1, and knit the extra stitch at the start of each right-side row.

Lovely for scarves, wraps and summer tops in a smooth cotton or linen yarn.
