Garter Stitch

The first stitch everyone learns: knit every row. A squishy, ridged fabric that lies flat and looks the same on both sides.

Cast on a multiple of 2 stitches. The pattern repeats every 2 rows. Read the chart from the bottom up. Right-side (odd) rows are read from right to left, wrong-side (even) rows from left to right.

Key

Purl on right-side rows; knit on wrong-side rows.

Instructions

Row 1 (RS)Knit.
Row 2 (WS)Knit.

Repeat rows 1–2 for the length you need.

Notes

Knit every row. The chart shows the wrong-side rows as purl bumps because that's how they look from the right side.

Each ridge is two rows, so count ridges instead of rows to keep track.

Garter stitch is wider and shorter than stocking stitch at the same gauge, so swatch it in garter.
