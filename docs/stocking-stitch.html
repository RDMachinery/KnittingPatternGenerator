Stocking Stitch

Smooth Vs on the right side and bumps on the back: the plain fabric of most jumpers. Called stockinette in the US.

Cast on a multiple of 2 stitches. The pattern repeats every 2 rows. Read the chart from the bottom up. Right-side (odd) rows are read from right to left, wrong-side (even) rows from left to right.

Key

Knit on right-side rows; purl on wrong-side rows.

Instructions

Row 1 (RS)Knit.
Row 2 (WS)Purl.

Repeat rows 1–2 for the length you need.

Notes

Knit the right-side rows and purl the wrong-side rows. In the round, knit every round.

The edges curl, so add a border of rib, garter or moss stitch if you want it to lie flat.
