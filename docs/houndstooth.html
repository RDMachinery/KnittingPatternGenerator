Houndstooth

The classic broken check, knitted small. A tiny 4-stitch repeat that's quick to memorise. Knitted in the round in two colours; lovely for hats, cowls and mittens.

Cast on a multiple of 4 stitches and join to work in the round. The pattern repeats every 4 rounds. Read the chart from the bottom up; every round is read from right to left.

Instructions

Round 1*k3 A, k1 B; repeat from * to end.
Round 2*k1 B, k3 A; repeat from * to end.
Round 3As round 2.
Round 4*k3 B, k1 A; repeat from * to end.

Repeat rounds 1–4 for the length you need.

Notes

Every round is knitted, so every round is read from right to left.

The longest float is only 3 stitches, so this is a good first stranded pattern.

Strong contrast shows the teeth best: try charcoal and cream, or black and white.
