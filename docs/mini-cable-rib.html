Mini Cable Rib

A rib with a tiny 2-stitch cable twisting up each knit column. All the stretch of ribbing, with more texture. Lovely for socks, hats and cuffs.

Cast on a multiple of 4 stitches. The pattern repeats every 4 rows. Read the chart from the bottom up. Right-side (odd) rows are read from right to left, wrong-side (even) rows from left to right.

Key

Knit on right-side rows; purl on wrong-side rows.
Purl on right-side rows; knit on wrong-side rows.
C2F: slip 1 stitch to a cable needle and hold at the front of the work, knit 1, then knit 1 from the cable needle. (Many knitters do this without a cable needle.)

Instructions

Row 1 (RS)*p1, C2F, p1; repeat from * to end.
Row 2 (WS)*k1, p2, k1; repeat from * to end.
Row 3 (RS)*p1, k2, p1; repeat from * to end.
Row 4 (WS)As row 2.

Repeat rows 1–4 for the length you need.

Notes

C2F crosses just two stitches, so you can work it without a cable needle: reach behind the first stitch and knit the second one through the back loop, then knit the first stitch, and slip both off together.

For a cuff, cast on a multiple of 4 and work in rib from the start.
