Horseshoe Cable

Two cables crossing away from each other, forming a horseshoe shape. Uses a back cross and a front cross on the same row.

This is a panel 12 stitches wide: work it between your other stitches, with a marker each side. It repeats every 8 rows. Read the chart from the bottom up. Right-side (odd) rows are read from right to left, wrong-side (even) rows from left to right.

Key

Knit on right-side rows; purl on wrong-side rows.
Purl on right-side rows; knit on wrong-side rows.
C4F: slip 2 stitches to a cable needle and hold at the front of the work, knit 2, then knit 2 from the cable needle.
C4B: slip 2 stitches to a cable needle and hold at the back of the work, knit 2, then knit 2 from the cable needle.

Instructions

Row 1 (RS)p2, k8, p2.
Row 2 (WS)k2, p8, k2.
Row 3 (RS)As row 1.
Row 4 (WS)As row 2.
Row 5 (RS)p2, C4B, C4F, p2.
Row 6 (WS)As row 2.
Row 7 (RS)As row 1.
Row 8 (WS)As row 2.

Repeat rows 1–8 for the length you need.

Notes

Work C4B first, then C4F, as the chart is read from right to left.

Swap them (C4F, then C4B) and the horseshoe turns upside down.
