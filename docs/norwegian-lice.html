Norwegian Lice

Tiny scattered dots of colour, called "lus" (lice) in Norway, from traditional Setesdal jumpers. An easy, relaxing introduction to two-colour knitting.

Cast on a multiple of 4 stitches and join to work in the round. The pattern repeats every 4 rounds. Read the chart from the bottom up; every round is read from right to left.

Yarns

 Yarn A: Red
 Yarn B: White

Instructions

Round 1*k3 A, k1 B; repeat from * to end.
Round 2Knit every stitch with yarn A.
Round 3*k1 A, k1 B, k2 A; repeat from * to end.
Round 4As round 2.

Repeat rounds 1–4 for the length you need.

Notes

Knitted in the round: every round is read from right to left.

On the rounds with no dots, leave the second colour hanging at the start of the round until you need it.

Lice is often used for the body and sleeves of a jumper, below a bolder yoke pattern.
