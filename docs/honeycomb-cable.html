Honeycomb Cable

An all-over cable texture of interlocking cells, like a honeycomb. Rich and warm for cushions, blankets and aran jumpers.

Cast on a multiple of 8 stitches. The pattern repeats every 8 rows. Read the chart from the bottom up. Right-side (odd) rows are read from right to left, wrong-side (even) rows from left to right.

Key

Knit on right-side rows; purl on wrong-side rows.
C4F: slip 2 stitches to a cable needle and hold at the front of the work, knit 2, then knit 2 from the cable needle.
C4B: slip 2 stitches to a cable needle and hold at the back of the work, knit 2, then knit 2 from the cable needle.

Instructions

Row 1 (RS)*C4B, C4F; repeat from * to end.
Row 2 (WS)Purl.
Row 3 (RS)Knit.
Row 4 (WS)As row 2.
Row 5 (RS)*C4F, C4B; repeat from * to end.
Row 6 (WS)As row 2.
Row 7 (RS)As row 3.
Row 8 (WS)As row 2.

Repeat rows 1–8 for the length you need.

Notes

Work the cables in the order shown, reading right to left: C4B then C4F on row 1, C4F then C4B on row 5.

Honeycomb pulls in a lot. Cast on noticeably more stitches than for plain knitting, and always knit a swatch.
