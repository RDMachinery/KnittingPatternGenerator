Twisted Rib

A 1×1 rib with every knit stitch twisted. Crisp, neat columns that hold their shape. A favourite for sock cuffs and traditional ganseys.

Cast on a multiple of 2 stitches. The pattern repeats every 2 rows. Read the chart from the bottom up. Right-side (odd) rows are read from right to left, wrong-side (even) rows from left to right.

Key

Purl on right-side rows; knit on wrong-side rows.
K1tbl: knit 1 through the back loop. This twists the stitch so it stands up crisp and neat (on a wrong-side row, p1tbl).

Instructions

Row 1 (RS)*k1tbl, p1; repeat from * to end.
Row 2 (WS)*k1, p1tbl; repeat from * to end.

Repeat rows 1–2 for the length you need.

Notes

Twisting the stitches makes the rib firmer and less stretchy than plain 1×1 rib.

On wrong-side rows the twisted stitches are purled through the back loop (p1tbl).
