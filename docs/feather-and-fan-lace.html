Feather and Fan Lace

The classic Shetland 'Old Shale' lace: gentle waves with a scalloped edge. Lovely for shawls, scarves and blanket borders.

Cast on a multiple of 18 stitches. The pattern repeats every 4 rows. Read the chart from the bottom up. Right-side (odd) rows are read from right to left, wrong-side (even) rows from left to right.

Key

Knit on right-side rows; purl on wrong-side rows.
Purl on right-side rows; knit on wrong-side rows.
Yarn over: take the yarn over the needle to make a new stitch and a small decorative hole.
K2tog: knit 2 stitches together. A right-leaning decrease (on a wrong-side row, p2tog).

Instructions

Row 1 (RS)Knit.
Row 2 (WS)Purl.
Row 3 (RS)*k2tog 3 times, (yo, k1) 6 times, k2tog 3 times; repeat from * to end.
Row 4 (WS)Knit.

Repeat rows 1–4 for the length you need.

Notes

Row 3 is balanced: six yarn overs replace the six stitches lost to the decreases, so you always end with the same number of stitches.

Knitting row 4 on the wrong side makes the little garter ridge that defines the pattern. For a smoother variant, purl row 4 instead.

Use a smooth, light-coloured yarn and block the finished piece to open up the lace.
