Double Moss Stitch

Little blocks of knit and purl in a chequerboard. A thick, flat, reversible texture (called moss stitch in the US).

Cast on a multiple of 4 stitches. The pattern repeats every 4 rows. Read the chart from the bottom up. Right-side (odd) rows are read from right to left, wrong-side (even) rows from left to right.

Key

Knit on right-side rows; purl on wrong-side rows.
Purl on right-side rows; knit on wrong-side rows.

Instructions

Row 1 (RS)*k2, p2; repeat from * to end.
Row 2 (WS)*k2, p2; repeat from * to end.
Row 3 (RS)*p2, k2; repeat from * to end.
Row 4 (WS)*p2, k2; repeat from * to end.

Repeat rows 1–4 for the length you need.

Notes

Work 2 rows of k2, p2 rib, then 2 rows with the knits and purls swapped.

It lies flat and looks the same on both sides, so it's a good choice for scarves and blankets.
