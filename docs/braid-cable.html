Braid Cable

Three strands plaited like a braid of hair. A classic aran cable that looks far harder than it is.

This is a panel 13 stitches wide: work it between your other stitches, with a marker each side. It repeats every 4 rows. Read the chart from the bottom up. Right-side (odd) rows are read from right to left, wrong-side (even) rows from left to right.

Key

Knit on right-side rows; purl on wrong-side rows.
Purl on right-side rows; knit on wrong-side rows.
C6F: slip 3 stitches to a cable needle and hold at the front of the work, knit 3, then knit 3 from the cable needle.
C6B: slip 3 stitches to a cable needle and hold at the back of the work, knit 3, then knit 3 from the cable needle.

Instructions

Row 1 (RS)p2, C6B, k3, p2.
Row 2 (WS)k2, p9, k2.
Row 3 (RS)p2, k3, C6F, p2.
Row 4 (WS)As row 2.

Repeat rows 1–4 for the length you need.

Notes

This is a panel: work its 13 stitches wherever you want the braid, with a stitch marker each side.

The two crossings take turns: C6B on the right-hand 6 stitches, then C6F on the left-hand 6.

Keep the crossings firm, and the braid stands out beautifully against the purl stitches each side.
